CostSauce

Price-drift radar for independent restaurants. The secret sauce is knowing your costs.

Problem

Independent restaurants lose margin silently: supplier prices drift weekly, menu prices don't. Owners track this in spreadsheets that break, or pay $199–$480/mo for enterprise food-cost platforms built for chains. In 2025, 82% of operators reported higher food costs (NRN/Technomic); owners on r/restaurateur describe "way too many nights fighting with food costs" and being quoted $480/mo for one location.

Product

Owners forward or photograph supplier invoices. CostSauce normalizes prices, ties them to recipes, and alerts the owner when a dish's food cost crosses target, with the price that fixes it. Working product exists (FastAPI + SQLite, seeded demo). No POS integration required; deliberately constrained scope undercuts the $199+ automation tier.

Business model

SaaS: $49 Starter / $99 Growth / $149 Pro (multi-location). Monthly billing, 14-day trial. 100 customers ≈ $7.9k MRR; 500 ≈ $39.5k MRR. Expansion: bookkeeper channel, OCR tier, POS add-ons.

Go to market

Founder-led in owner communities (r/restaurateur, r/restaurantowners, FB groups), a free food-cost calculator as the wedge, restaurant bookkeepers as a distribution channel, design partners for proof.

Risks (we name them ourselves)

  • Affordable competitors exist (Backbar, meez, RCC); our defensibility is owner-side drift alerting + radically simpler onboarding, not recipe costing alone.
  • Independent restaurants are financially stressed; churn risk is real. Mitigation: weekly value email, seasonal pause, month-to-month pricing.
  • OCR/POS automation is where incumbents charge $199+; we defer it rather than fake it.
