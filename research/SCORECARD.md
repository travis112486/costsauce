# Tournament Scorecard — Phase 2

Each candidate pain is scored 0–10 on 7 dimensions by the judge (orchestrator),
then attacked by a skeptic pass. Weighted total = final score.

| Dimension | Weight | What earns a 10 |
|---|---|---|
| Pain intensity | 2.0 | Verbatim quotes show emotional/financial bleeding ("nightmare", "$ lost", "hours wasted") |
| Frequency & prevalence | 1.5 | Multiple independent threads/communities, recent (2025-26), high engagement |
| Willingness to pay | 2.0 | People already pay for bad workarounds (VAs, templates, overpriced incumbents) |
| Underserved gap | 1.5 | Named incumbents with quoted weaknesses, price umbrella, or no real solution |
| Solo-launch feasibility | 1.5 | One person can build + ship core value in weeks; no two-sided cold start; no regulation wall |
| Buyer reachability | 1.0 | Obvious channel to first 50 customers this month (communities, search, marketplaces) |
| Evidence quality | 0.5 | Every claim has a fetched URL; quotes verbatim; recency confirmed |

Skeptic pass: top-2 candidates each get a refutation attempt — strongest survives.
