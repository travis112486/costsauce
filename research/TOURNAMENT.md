## Finalist reasoning

**C1 — Restaurant COGS/recipe costing for independent restaurants. WINNER.**
- Explicit price umbrella: owner asks for "<$200/mo" while evaluating MarginEdge at $480/mo, xtraCHEF $300–350/mo, Sculpture $400/mo (all quoted, fetched URLs).
- Strongest PMF signal in the whole corpus: owners *built spreadsheets, watched them break, then one built a $10/mo app himself*. Spreadsheet duct-tape = validated workflow + unserved budget.
- Recurring usage (weekly invoices/price changes), recurring revenue, zero cold-start trap, unregulated, boring, reachable buyers (r/restaurateur, r/restaurantowners, FB groups).
- Buildable MVP this month: invoice/price tracking → recipe costing → plate-cost dashboard + price-drift alerts.

**A1 — GBP suspension rescue. RUNNER-UP (killed at finalist stage).**
- Highest raw intensity ($35k/mo revenue loss quotes), but: (1) the outcome customers pay for — reinstatement — is controlled by Google's opaque appeal process, not by us; (2) episodic, insurance-like usage = weak engagement loop and hard month-1 proof; (3) core deliverable skews service/agency, not product. Monitoring+backup is a feature, not a company.

## Skeptic pass
Skeptic agent verdict on C1: **WOUND** (research/skeptic-c1.md). Affordable competitors exist (Backbar $0–149 bars, meez $24–119, RCC $29/mo, MarketMan $199+ automation floor); Toast/Clover integration is certification-gated; bars are occupied. Required pivot: narrow to **invoice price-drift radar + recipe recosting for food-heavy single-location independents**, no POS/EDI promises in base tier. **Re-decision: C1 SURVIVES with pivot and remains the winner** — the drift-alert wedge is the precise gap incumbents charge $199+/mo for, and A1's original weaknesses (Google-controlled outcomes, episodic usage) still hold. Business design revised accordingly (business/BUSINESS_MODEL.md).
