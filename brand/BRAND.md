# CostSauce — Brand Guide

## Name
**CostSauce** — "The secret sauce is knowing your costs."

## Voice
Plain-spoken kitchen English. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. We talk like a line cook turned accountant, never like a vendor. Banned words: *revolutionize, empower, seamless, leverage, unlock, elevate, streamline, journey, game-changer, delve, landscape.* We say "your chicken got 14% more expensive," not "leverage real-time procurement intelligence."
