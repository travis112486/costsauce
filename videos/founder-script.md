**01 (0:00–0:10)**
VO: "I didn't start CostSauce because I love software. I started it because I kept reading the same sentence from restaurant owners."
CAP: "I've spent way too many nights fighting with food costs."

**02 (0:10–0:24)**
VO: "One owner wrote that his spreadsheet worked great, until it didn't. The formulas broke. His team broke them. He was the only one who could fix them. That sentence has thousands of upvotes' worth of pain behind it."
CAP: "The formulas got fragile, my team broke them constantly."

**03 (0:24–0:38)**
VO: "Here's the thing. The fix exists. MarginEdge, xtraCHEF, MarketMan. Real products. But they're priced for chains. One owner was quoted four hundred and eighty dollars a month. For one location."
CAP: $480/mo — quoted to a one-location owner

**04 (0:38–0:50)**
VO: "So the independent places, the taqueria, the bistro, the burger joint your town would miss, they get a spreadsheet and a prayer."
CAP: 412,498 independent locations in the US (2025)

**05 (0:50–1:04)**
VO: "CostSauce does one job. You send it your supplier invoices. It watches every price, ties them to your recipes, and taps you on the shoulder when a dish stops making money. Before the month eats the difference."
CAP: Invoices in. Drift alerts out.

**06 (1:04–1:16)**
VO: "It's forty-nine dollars a month because that's what the math allows when you skip the enterprise features nobody at one location uses. No POS project. No annual contract. Photo, prices, alerts."
CAP: $49/mo. No contract.

**07 (1:16–1:28)**
VO: "If you run a restaurant, try the free food-cost calculator on the site. If it tells you something you didn't know, imagine what your invoices would."
CAP: Free calculator → costsauce.com

**08 (1:28–1:34)**
VO: "CostSauce. Know your costs. Keep your margin."
CAP: CostSauce
