## How it works
H2: Three steps. None of them is a spreadsheet.
1. Send the invoice. Photograph it, forward the email, or drop in the Sysco CSV. Price history builds itself.
2. See every plate's cost. Your recipes meet current prices, not last quarter's. Food-cost % per item, against your target.
3. Get tapped on the shoulder. When an ingredient drifts past your threshold, CostSauce names the menu items that need a reprice, and the price that fixes them.

## FAQ
- Do I need a new POS? No. CostSauce sits between your invoices and your menu. Paper, email, or CSV in; alerts out.
- How is this different from a spreadsheet? The spreadsheet waits for you to notice. CostSauce notices for you, and it doesn't break when your prep cook adds a row.
- What if my vendor names everything weird? "CHKN BRST BNLS" maps to chicken breast once. After that it sticks.
- Can I export? CSV of everything, on Growth and up. Your data is yours on every plan.
